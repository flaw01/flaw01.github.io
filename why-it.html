Why IT?

Why I chose IT

During high school I had computer science as a school subject, and it immediately became my favourite subject. The programming assignments were a lot of fun to make, because I really liked the problem solving and trying to find the best solution instead of just a working one. I liked it so much so that I always added extra stuff to everything. This made me explore programming on my own, and that was also enjoyable, mainly because it feels great when your code finally works the way you want it to. After working on various projects, including a rhythm game, optimization algorithms and tools to speed up annoying tasks, I’m still really motivated to develop software and push my problem solving to the limit. Programming to me is a fun way of solving problems in creative and efficient ways. I’d really like to learn and become a competent developer that people can rely upon and make software that will really make an impact on a business or people’s lives. I would also really like creating tools that can help smaller communities that don’t have a lot of technically competent people to help them. I feel like HBO-ICT at the HZ is a great programme to help me complete these goals.

Why HZ?

I chose the HZ specifically because it's a university of applied sciences, meaning I have the chance to learn a lot by gaining experience in the real world, instead of just learning theory, or working on projects that have no real meaning outside of getting a grade. Another element that has drawn me towards the HZ, is that it's close enough to home, so I can still live with my parents. I was worried I would find it difficult to live alone or away from my family, but luckily I won't have to.
