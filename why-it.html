Why IT?

What made me choose IT

During high school I had computer science as a school subject, and it immediately became my favorite subject. The programming assignments were a lot of fun to make, so much so that I always added extra stuff to everything. This made me explore programming on my own, and that was also really enjoyable. Programming to me is a really fun way of solving problems in creative and efficient ways. I chose IT because i'd really like to learn to become an IT professional and solve people's or businesses' problems in a way that I enjoy.
