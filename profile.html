Who am I?

About me

My name is Tias. I am Dutch and 18 years old. I have been living in a small town called Dreischor my entire life.

My education

I completed VWO in high school with an NG and NT profile, HBO-ICT is my first programme at a university (of applied sciences).

My future after the programme

Right now I'm thinking of a software development job, because I love programming and working on projects and making them as close to perfect as I can get them. This can obviously change because maybe I'll find something that interests me even more during this study. I hope that in 4 years I'll just be competent enough to be able to confidently work on my own projects while also being able of doing a good job at whatever work I'll do.
