My goals & SWOT analysis

Thursday, 24/09/2026

Hello, during class today we made a SWOT (strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats) analysis and we laid out a couple goals and a mission based on this analysis. In this blog post, I will share the analysis, goals and mission.

SWOT Analysis

STRENGTHS

  • I'm great at being precise and doing a job well when I'm tasked with doing something, I don’t rush it.
  • I don’t give up easily and I’m persistent to learn what is needed to succeed.
  • I’m good at adapting to completely new or unusual situations, focusing on what is important.

WEAKNESSES

  • I struggle working with others because I like doing things my own way and that’s not always possible.
  • I procrastinate when uninterested.
  • Sometimes I commit too early to working on a solution that’s not optimal.

OPPORTUNITIES

  • Learn and become an IT professional.
  • Build real work experience.
  • Build a strong resume.

THREATS

  • Companies hiring fewer IT workers.
  • Some knowledge becoming outdated.
  • I might fail to specialize in something that doesn’t have a lot of professionals in that field.

Goals

I chose 1 point each from my strengths and weaknesses and turned it into a SMART goal.

Goal 1 (Strength)

I will use my ability to adapt to learn as many unique IT related skills as possible, that can later help me work on projects.

Every weekend I will assess what I want to learn that week, and work on that for at least 4 hours a week. By the end of the week I will decide if I need more time to learn that specific thing and continue working on that for another week, or start next week off with something new.

At the end of the semester I will evaluate what I learned and if I spent my time on useful things.

Goal 2 (Weakness)

I will stop procrastinating by making sure I prioritize my studies over my hobbies especially when necessary.

Every time I want to spend time doing something that isn’t productive I will evaluate if there is an assignment I need to work on first, and I will only allow myself to do what I actually want to do after completing or spending a realistic time on that assignment.

By the end of the semester I will evaluate if I held up my goal, or if I procrastinated anyway.

My mission this year

I am committed to growing as an IT professional by continuously learning new and unique skills that strengthen my ability to work on projects. Each week, I will intentionally choose what to focus on and dedicate time to developing that skill. Alongside this, I will prioritize my studies by actively managing procrastination and making sure important assignments come before hobbies. Throughout the year, I will evaluate my progress to ensure I’m learning effectively and staying disciplined. My mission is to build strong habits that support both my personal growth and academic success.
