WORK IN PROGRESS

High school

The first time I learned programming was in computer science class in high school. I had a lot of fun learning the basics of Python and SQL, and even some C programming with an Arduino. The year after we had to make a game in JavaScript, for which I made a game consisting of small tasks or minigames, that keeps going faster and faster and the goal is to keep up as long as possible. You can play and view the terrible code here.

In that same year mes and Simon Kappetijn (who is also in this class) made our own rhythm game for our graduation project (profielwerkstuk). It was quite a simple game, press keys to catch the falling arrows at the right time. There was a twist though, there were multiple groups of participants who received feedback in different ways. We then collected the data and found out whether positive feedback actually improves your performance compared to more negative feedabk. To do that we learned how to use Godot Engine, which was honestly tough but it turned out pretty alright. You can watch or skip through the video below if you'd like to see how it turned out.
