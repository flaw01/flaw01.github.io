My direction in IT

Thursday, 09/10/2026

This week at PCO, I was tasked with finding a direction that appeals to me, and to look that up and research it. I filled in a table about my chosen direction, software development, and I wrote a text about where I see myself in the future. I will share these in the form of a Q&A, in this blog post.

My IT direction: Q&A

What is your chosen direction?

I chose software development.

What do you do in this field?

You build software that solves real problems. This includes designing applications, writing code, testing and debugging, collaborating with others and deploying and maintaining software.

Which skills are important?

Some important skills that a strong developer needs are:

  • Programming fundamentals
  • Problem solving
  • Debugging
  • Communication
  • Knowledge of frameworks

Ofcourse there are many more important skills, but these are a few of them.

What are some example features?

There are a lot of different types of developers you can turn into, for example:

  • Junior Software Developer / Engineer
  • Front-End Developer
  • Back-End Developer
  • Full-Stack Developer
  • Mobile App Developer

Why does this appeal to me?

I really like programming and building things, and software development allows me to combine the two. I enjoy the problem solving aspect a lot, because it feels great when you finally solve a big or annoying problem. It's also really cool to be able to create meaningful applications that have real impact to me or to others.

Where do you see yourself in 4 years? What function? What type of business? What have you learned and achieved?

In 4 years I see myself working at some type of tech company as a back-end developer. I hope I will be able to use the experience from internships and minors to be able to solve problems efficiently and work together with others, and communicate clearly with my teammates and the customer to create exactly what they want. I also see myself helping out at my parents' practice on the IT side, making tools that will make them more efficient in their work. I will also be working on my own projects, possibly together with friends, for fun and to gain experience.
