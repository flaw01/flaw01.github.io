My direction in IT

Thursday, 09/10/2026

This week at PCO, I was tasked with finding a direction that appeals to me, and to look that up and research it. I had to choose between software development and data science. I filled in a table about my chosen direction, software development, and I wrote a text about where I see myself in the future. I will share these in the form of a Q&A, in this blog post.

My IT direction: Q&A

What is your chosen direction?

I chose software development.

What do you do in this field?

You build software that solves real problems. This includes designing applications, writing code, testing and debugging, collaborating with others and deploying and maintaining software.

You didn't end up choosing data science, but what do you do in that field?

You collect, clean and analyze large amounts of data to discover patterns and gain useful insights. You still use programming like in software development, but for a different purpose. You also use statistics and sometimes machine learning to make predictions and solve problems. You also visualize data to help businesses make better decisions.

Which skills are important?

Some important skills that a strong developer needs are:

  • Programming fundamentals
  • Problem solving
  • Debugging
  • Communication
  • Knowledge of frameworks

Ofcourse there are many more important skills, but these are a few of them.

What are some example features?

There are a lot of different types of developers you can turn into, for example:

  • Junior Software Developer / Engineer
    An entry-level developer who writes, tests and fixes code while gaining experience.
  • Front-End Developer
    Builds the parts of websites and applications that users see and interact with.
  • Back-End Developer
    Works on the behind-the-scenes functionality of applications, such as servers, databases and APIs.
  • Full-Stack Developer
    Works on both the front-end and back-end of applications.
  • Mobile App Developer
    Designs and builds applications for mobile devices.

Why does this appeal to me?

I really like programming and building things, and software development allows me to combine the two. I enjoy the problem solving aspect a lot, because it feels great when you finally solve a big or annoying problem. It's also really cool to be able to create meaningful applications that have real impact to me or to others.

Why not choose data science?

Data science is definitely an interesting field, and choosing between software development and data science was not an easy choice. I do enjoy working with data and finding patterns. However, in the end I do simply enjoy building something more than mainly analyzing data. I like writing code, solving problems and seeing something I've worked on come to life. Data science also involves a lot of statistics and mathematics, and honestly I am not the biggest fan of either of those. I think it wouldn't excite me like programming and creating software does. That's why I think software development simply fits my interests better.

Where do you see yourself in 4 years? What function? What type of business? What have you learned and achieved?

In 4 years I see myself working at some type of tech company as a back-end developer. I hope I will be able to use the experience from internships and minors to be able to solve problems efficiently and work together with others, and communicate clearly with my teammates and the customer to create exactly what they want. I also see myself helping out at my parents' practice on the IT side, making tools that will make them more efficient in their work. I will also be working on my own projects, possibly together with friends, for fun and to gain experience.
